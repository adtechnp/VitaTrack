Privacy-First Summary

VitaTrack is engineered with an on-device, local-first architecture. We do not require an account, we do not operate remote servers to harvest your health entries, and your personal wellness records (weight, calories, water logs, steps) are stored strictly on your smartphone.

1. Overview & Commitment

This Privacy Policy explains how VitaTrack ("we", "our", or "the App"), identified by the Android package name com.adtech.vitatrack, handles information when you install and use our mobile application.

We are firmly committed to user privacy. We recognize that health, fitness, body composition, and dietary logs are deeply sensitive personal data. Accordingly, VitaTrack does not collect, transmit, or monetize your health information.

2. Information We Process

When you use VitaTrack, the application processes the following types of information on your device:

A. Health and Fitness Metrics

  • Body Measurements: Height, weight logs, weight goals, and calculated Body Mass Index (BMI).
  • Nutritional Logs: Meals, food names, estimated calories, and macronutrient breakdowns (carbohydrates, protein, and fat).
  • Hydration Entries: Water intake amounts (in milliliters or fluid ounces) and daily fluid intake targets.
  • Habit Streaks: Daily participation records and milestone badges in the 7-day "Small Wins" challenge.

B. Physical Activity & Sensor Data

  • Step Counts: Raw step events detected by your device's hardware step sensor or accelerometer.
  • Derived Activity Metrics: Estimated distance walked and calories burned calculated directly on your device.

C. User Profile Preferences

  • Unit Settings: Metric (kg, cm, ml) or Imperial (lbs, ft/in, oz) preferences.
  • Theme & Notification Settings: Dark mode selection, reminder schedules for water and weigh-ins.

3. On-Device Local Storage

All health, nutrition, hydration, and activity entries described above are saved exclusively in a local SQLite database (managed via Drift) and Android SharedPreferences stored within the private, sandboxed internal storage of your device.

VitaTrack does not operate a cloud backend, external user database, or centralized tracking server. Your data does not leave your smartphone unless you explicitly initiate an operating system level export or backup.

4. Android Device Permissions & Usage

In accordance with Google Play Developer Policies and Android system requirements, below is a complete, transparent disclosure of the permissions requested by VitaTrack:

Permission Purpose & Justification
ACTIVITY_RECOGNITION Required on Android 10 (API level 29) and above to read step detector and step counter hardware events while the app is active or running in the background. Sensor events are processed locally and discarded once daily totals are tabulated.
HIGH_SAMPLING_RATE_SENSORS Enables the application to sample pedometer sensors accurately without losing step counts during brisk walking or outdoor exercise.
RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED Enables the app to re-register your local reminder alarms (such as hydration reminders and daily weigh-in alerts) after your device reboots. No data is transmitted during boot.
POST_NOTIFICATIONS Allows the app to display scheduled local notifications for hydration reminders and milestone achievements. All notifications originate on-device.

5. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

VitaTrack is free to download and use. To fund continuous software maintenance and feature updates, the application displays banner advertisements provided by Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK).

Google AdMob may collect and process certain non-personal device information in order to serve contextual or personalized advertisements in compliance with applicable laws and Google policies:

  • Advertising Identifiers: Google Advertising ID (AAID on Android) or Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA).
  • Device & Telemetry Data: IP address (which may be used to estimate coarse/general location), device manufacturer, model, operating system version, and mobile network carrier.
  • Ad Performance Metrics: Ad impressions, click-through rates, and diagnostic data related to ad serving.
Crucial Notice Regarding Health Data

Google AdMob does NOT have access to your private health records, calorie logs, water intake, or weight measurements stored inside VitaTrack's local SQLite database. We never share or expose your health data to advertising SDKs.

For more information about how Google collects and uses information for advertising, please review:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services

You can opt out of personalized advertising at any time through your Android system settings by visiting Settings > Google > Ads and selecting "Delete advertising ID" or resetting your advertising identifier.

6. Data Sharing, Selling & Disclosure

We firmly commit to the following principles:

  • We do not sell, rent, lease, or trade your personal health data to third-party data brokers, marketers, or analytics aggregators.
  • We do not upload your nutritional or fitness logs to external servers.
  • We only disclose data if required by enforceable law or court subpoena, though because we do not maintain a cloud database of your entries, we possess no remote copies of your health logs to disclose.

7. Data Retention & User Deletion Rights

You maintain full autonomy over your data at all times:

  • In-App Deletion: You can edit or delete individual logs (meals, water entries, weight records) directly within the application.
  • Complete Wipe: You can purge all records from the device by clearing the app's storage via Android Settings > Apps > VitaTrack > Storage > Clear Storage / Clear Data.
  • Uninstalling: Completely uninstalling VitaTrack from your device permanently removes its private local SQLite database and all saved preferences.

8. Children’s Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K)

VitaTrack is intended for general audiences aged 13 and older (or 16 where required by regional European law). We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal data from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately, and we will guide you on purging any local device data.

9. Data Security

Because all personal entries reside directly on your hardware, your data is protected by the built-in operating system security sandbox of Android. Unauthorized third-party apps installed on your device cannot access VitaTrack's private database directory without your operating system being compromised (e.g., rooted).

10. Changes to This Policy & Contact Us

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in app functionality or regulatory obligations. Updates will be published on this website with a revised "Effective Date" at the top of this document.
