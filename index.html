Privacy by Design

Your health data belongs to you. Not to our servers.

Unlike traditional fitness platforms that upload your weight, meals, and private habits to the cloud, VitaTrack operates entirely on your device using local SQLite (Drift) storage.

On-Device Storage

Every logged meal, water drop, and step count is written to a private local SQLite database on your hardware.

Full Offline Mode

No internet connection needed. Track your health in airplane mode, outdoors, or in remote workouts seamlessly.

Zero Forced Accounts

No passwords to remember, no email signups, and zero third-party profile scraping.

Instant Data Deletion

You can wipe all data with one tap in your profile or by clearing app storage anytime.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about the app, permissions, and publishing.

Is VitaTrack free to use?
Yes! VitaTrack is completely free to download and use. All core fitness tracking features — including steps, nutrition, hydration, and the 7-day challenge — are fully accessible to every user without any subscription.
Why does VitaTrack request Activity Recognition permission?
VitaTrack uses Android's ACTIVITY_RECOGNITION permission and native hardware step detector sensors to count your steps accurately while conserving your phone's battery. This sensor data is processed strictly on your device and is never shared or transmitted anywhere.
Does VitaTrack upload my health measurements to the cloud?
No. We believe your health metrics belong solely to you. VitaTrack uses an on-device SQLite database via Drift. We do not operate remote servers to store your weight, meals, steps, or personal notes.
Can I switch between Metric and Imperial units?
Yes! You can toggle between Metric (kg, cm, ml) and Imperial (lbs, feet/inches, oz) anytime from your Profile settings. Your historical numbers will adapt seamlessly across all screens.
How does advertising work in VitaTrack?
To support continuous development while keeping the app free, VitaTrack integrates Google AdMob for banner ads. Google AdMob may use device advertising identifiers (AAID) in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy. For complete details, see our Privacy Policy.
